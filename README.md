## About

A production landing page, not a demo. It presents a real service to real prospects: AI-accelerated development backed by senior architects, aimed at pre-seed and seed AI-native startups, non-technical founders, and teams preparing for investment.

The page had to do a specific job — make the case that speed and engineering integrity aren't a trade-off — and do it in three languages for three markets.

## What's in it

- **Trilingual** — English, German and Ukrainian, switchable in place
- **Nine sections** — hero, benefits, expertise, audience, partnership, process, portfolio, FAQ, contacts
- **Six-step process breakdown** — from discovery through vibe check, architecture, iteration loop, QA and deployment
- **Animated tech marquee** and motion throughout
- **Interactive FAQ** and a case study block
- **Single conversion path** — every CTA leads to a project vibe check

## Stack

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Vercel`

Built with AI-assisted development.

## My role

Design Engineer — design and front-end implementation.

I designed the layout, type system and visual direction, then built the page myself in React. Copy was written by our content team; I shaped how it lands on the page — hierarchy, rhythm, what gets weight and what gets cut.
